# Story and script

The user brings the idea. Your job: turn it into a script that has the structure of the hits, without changing what the user wants to tell.
Propose, explain why in one line per change, and build only after the user says yes.

## Structure that works
1. **The rule** in the first line, said as if normal: "In this town, ..." (or "In this school / world ...").
2. **How it works**: one concrete example, with a number.
3. **The stakes**: a small case and a huge case of the rule.
4. **Town life** under the rule (a legend, a habit, what people do).
5. **"And me"**: the hero's place in it, often the exception ("... except me").
6. **A person** the viewer will meet again, with details that return later (a name, an item, a habit).
7. **The problem**, then **tries that fail** (three is classic), then **the last chance**.
8. **The big moment**, then **the twist**: an ending that flips the start or reveals what the planted details meant.
This is a template, not a law: short stories can merge steps. What matters: one rule, one hero, escalation, a payoff.

## Writing rules
- One sentence per line in `script.txt`; 3.5-4.7 spoken words per second; estimate length at about 3.7 words/s before recording.
- Write numbers as words ("eighteen thirty-one"); digits make text-to-speech slow and captions wrong.
- Names and exact numbers over vague words; dialogue lines in quotes make scenes ("What is going on?" I said.).
- Every sentence should be showable: if the viewer cannot see it, rewrite it or plan an insert (a sign, a phone, a clock).
- Plant 2-3 details early that come back. End on something that invites a comment or a part 2.

## Hook check (before anything is built)
- Which character is big on screen in the first 1.5 s, and does the first sentence show the rule (or the premise) on them?
- Is the first picture clean and readable, not an empty place or a dark frame?

## Checkpoint
Show the user: the script, the estimated length, which places and characters it needs, and the 2-3 planted details. Build after their yes.
